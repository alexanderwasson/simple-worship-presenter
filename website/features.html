Features

Built around how a service actually runs.

Simple Worship Presenter is a worship presentation tool, not a general slide deck builder. The workflow is service plans, songs, media and a live display — in that order.

▤

Service plans

Group presentations into a plan for a date, add notes, duplicate the plan for next week and launch straight into presenter mode.

♪

Song library with sections

Store lyrics as Verse, Chorus, Bridge and custom sections. Reorder sections, repeat them, and generate correctly spaced slides with your chosen line count.

©

Credits and CCLI

Record author, artist, copyright and CCLI number per song, and add the church CCLI licence to the workspace footer.

▧

Media library

Upload images, video and audio. Set any image as a slide or presentation background without re-uploading it every week.

▷

Live audience display

Opens as a separate window for the projector. Blank and restore it, jump to any slide, and go fullscreen with a keystroke.

▯

Mobile remote

Pair a phone by QR code and run the service from anywhere in the room. Large one-handed controls, live slide previews, and instant revocation.

↶

Real editing

Undo and redo, duplicate slides, reorder them, toggle 16:9 and 4:3, and edit text with autosave.

On song lyrics and Bible translations. Simple Worship Presenter does not ship any copyrighted song lyrics or Bible translations. You bring your own content and the rights to use it. Where you hold a CCLI licence, record it in Settings so it can appear on your slides.
