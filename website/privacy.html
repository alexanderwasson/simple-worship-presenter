Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026.

What we collect

On the hosted service we store the account details you provide (name, email, a hashed password) and the content you create: presentations, slides, songs, service plans and uploaded media. Server logs record request method, path, status and timing for operational purposes.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not use your lyrics, media or account content to train AI models.
  • We do not read your presentations.
  • We do not send marketing email, and we do not use your email for anything other than the account and service.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • We do not require an account with us to self-host the software.
  • A self-hosted instance sends nothing to us at all.

Cookies and third-party services

The application sets one cookie: a signed, HttpOnly session cookie that keeps you signed in. There are no advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party analytics.

The website and the application load fonts from Google Fonts. That request is made by your browser directly to Google and is governed by Google's own privacy policy; if you prefer not to make that request, the pages still work with your system fonts.

Who we share information with

We share what we hold only as far as it takes to run the service:

  • the infrastructure provider that hosts the servers and storage for app.simpleworshippresenter.com;
  • any service we use to send essential account or service email;
  • anyone else, where you have asked us to, or agreed to it.

We do not share your content or account details with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone who wants to market to you.

Legal requests, safety and abuse

We may disclose information where we reasonably believe the law requires it, or where it is necessary to protect the service, its users or another person from harm, fraud or abuse. Where we are legally permitted to tell you about such a request, we will.

AI disclosure

Parts of the software itself were produced with AI-assisted development and reviewed by a human before release. That applies to source code and interface copy only. AI is not used to generate, process or store the worship content you enter. If you later choose a feature that generates text for you, it will be clearly labelled and optional.

Where your data lives

On the hosted service, data is stored on the servers running app.simpleworshippresenter.com. When you self-host, your data lives entirely on the machine you control — database and uploaded files are stored in a single directory you configure and back up.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account from Settings. That permanently removes your presentations, songs, service plans, media and files, and releases the storage they occupied. Backups may retain data for a limited period before they rotate.

Bible translation data

The application includes the World English Bible (WEB), which is in the public domain. The name World English Bible is a trademark of eBible.org. The text is installed on the server and shared by every account — it is not copied into your account and it does not count against your storage allowance. Looking up a passage or searching the text makes no request to any external Bible service. When you generate slides from a passage, the resulting slides become your content. Attribution and the source URL are recorded with the translation.

The mobile remote

The remote is a web page, not an installed app. Pairing issues a short-lived, single-use code that is exchanged for a device token; the code itself contains no account credentials. The server keeps a record of paired devices so you can see and revoke them. Presentation state — the current slide, the running order and whether the output is blanked — is held on the server for the length of the session so the phone, the control window and the projector agree. Disconnecting a device or ending the session stops that access immediately.

Hosted versus self-hosted

Hosted service. Alexander Wasson operates the infrastructure behind app.simpleworshippresenter.com and can, as the operator, access data stored on it. Do not assume otherwise.

Self-hosted. If you install the software yourself, the administrator of that instance controls the server, the data and the retention. This privacy policy describes the hosted service; a self-hosted instance is operated by whoever installed it. The software does not send your presentations, media or account details to the developer's server. The only outbound request in normal operation is the optional update check, which the administrator can disable.

Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text. Sessions use signed, HttpOnly cookies. Every protected request is authorised server-side and scoped to your account. Rate limits are applied to sign-in endpoints. We recommend HTTPS for every deployment.

No method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will tell you and take reasonable steps to deal with it, and will notify you or the relevant authority where the law requires it.

Children

The hosted service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact the site owner and we will remove it.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data or its deletion by contacting the site owner. Churches using a self-hosted instance should contact their own administrator. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under applicable data protection law; contact the site owner to exercise them.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. Material changes will be noted on this page with an updated date.
